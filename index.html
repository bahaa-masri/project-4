About Us

Established in 1966, the Fernand Hosri Group is a multinational company serving the Middle East region, with established offices in Lebanon, the UAE, and Saudi Arabia. Over the years, the group has expanded its presence across various sectors in both local and international markets, focusing on Security, Telecommunications, Building Automation & Communication, Renewable Energy, B2B and B2C Distribution, as well as Insurance consultancy and brokerage.

Fire Protection

We are experts in fire protection solutions including alarm systems, sprinkler systems, fire extinguishers, and emergency lights. Our team will ensure that all installations comply with local regulations and the highest international standards. With us you can rest assured and focus on running your operation smoothly.

Security Systems

We provide state of the art security systems, including CCTV surveillance, access control, intruder alarms, and intercom systems. Our solutions enhance the safety and security of our client premises.

Maintenance Support and Civil Defence Licencing

EYWA enables women to recreate what they can be and what it is they can accomplish through assisting them to proper education. Increased literacy and status will allow those women to raise better generations and enhance the quality of our human resources.
