About Us

Established in 1966, the Fernand Hosri Group is a multinational company serving the Middle East region, with established offices in Lebanon, the UAE, and Saudi Arabia. Over the years, the group has expanded its presence across various sectors in both local and international markets, focusing on Security, Telecommunications, Building Automation & Communication, Renewable Energy, B2B and B2C Distribution, as well as Insurance consultancy and brokerage.
